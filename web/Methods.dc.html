Three rules

  • A missing price stays missing. Nothing on the board is ever filled in, and a game the model refuses to price is shown as not priced, never as a pass.
  • A number is checked against what happened before it is believed. Where a check can only rule a method out, it is never read as ruling one in.
  • A market reaches the card only after it has been measured against real prices and a person has approved it in writing. The card recommends; it never places a bet.

The full ratings table, every team in Division I, is the Ratings view of the Season page. Each game's page shows the steps as they were applied to that game.
