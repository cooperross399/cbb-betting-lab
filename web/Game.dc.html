Edge is our chance minus the chance the price implies: for the moneyline with the book's margin removed across both sides, for the spread and total against the posted price itself, which overstates the book by its margin. A positive edge is not a profit: the measured record is on All results, and no measured market shows a demonstrated edge.

How to read this

  • The win chance includes overtime. Projected scores are the means of the model's score distribution, so they include overtime too.
  • A bet is a frozen opinion the card selected on an allowlisted market. A pass is an opinion the card priced and did not select. "Not priced" means nothing was judged.
  • Every step above is the lab's own number; the second opinion is a published ratings table blended in at half weight, and it is named in the lab's documentation, not here.
