## The stopping rule, checked weekly

`docs/when_this_ends.md` ends this project early if the correction grows large enough that a plausible edge could not clear it on a season's sample. At **130** cumulative hypotheses the correction is **x1.81**, so separating a **+5%** edge from zero needs **5,044** bets against **1,537** uncorrected.

A season supplies on the order of 23,000 settled opinions, so there is headroom. Against that supply the correction only bites at **1,466,970,562,662** cumulative hypotheses — which is the honest shape of a Bonferroni correction rather than a reassurance: its z grows like the square root of the log of the family size, so **the cumulative count is not what will end this project.** The decision date and the sample floor are.

Against the 10,000-opinion floor the correction bites at **86,909** cumulative hypotheses, and that is the number worth watching.

The floor below which the test **did not run** is 10,000 settled opinions across 2,000 distinct games. Below it the verdict is not a number.

This arithmetic assumes independent bets of unit variance, which this sport does not supply — so this is a lower bound on the bets needed and therefore an optimistic read of the remaining headroom.
