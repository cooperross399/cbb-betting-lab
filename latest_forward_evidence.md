# Forward evidence

> **Markets excluded from this report.** 14 player market(s) are EXCLUDED from the report above and from every interval in it: player_assists, player_blocks, player_blocks_steals, player_double_double, player_first_basket, player_points, player_points_assists, player_points_rebounds, player_pra, player_rebounds, player_rebounds_assists, player_steals, player_threes, player_turnovers. No wager census has reconciled in this process, so this run is not allowed to grade a prop. That is an exclusion, not a pass, an avoid or a no-value call, and the rows stay in the ledger ungraded. The team markets above are unaffected.

**Forward evidence cannot be back-dated.** Historical prices can be bought at any time; a night the pipeline did not freeze and settle is a night of clean out-of-sample data gone permanently, and in this sport a night is up to 200 games. Everything below was frozen before tip and settled afterwards against the box score. Nothing here was priced with knowledge of a result.

**107,823 frozen opinions in the ledger.** 73,102 are resolved wagers carrying a readable price and are measured below. 34,721 are **unsettleable**, 0 are **void** — a bet that never existed rather than one that returned its stake — and 0 settled at a price this lab cannot read. None of those three is a zero, and none of them enters any interval.

Every interval is corrected across **200 hypotheses ever tested**, from the experiment ledger's cumulative count rather than this table's row count.

Intervals are clustered by game **and** by day, and the wider of the two is reported. One game supplies a moneyline, a spread, a total, two team totals and a dozen props: a 100-game Tuesday is not 1,500 independent observations.

## Opinions and bets are reported separately, always

**73,102 opinions** — every settled row, whatever its edge — and **24,266 bets** — the rows clearing the +2.0% edge declared in advance. Both are printed every time, because mixing them flatters whichever is worse.

## Reachability

**This ledger does not carry price survival**, so reachability is unmeasured here rather than measured and found fine. A number reported without it is a number about prices that may not have existed by the time anybody could take them.

## Futures

No settled futures in the ledger. When there are, they appear here and only here: a futures stake is tied up for months and settles on a different clock, and **no futures return is ever folded into a headline computed over game bets.**

Every figure above carries its sample size. An interval including zero is **no demonstrated edge** — not promising, not trending positive, not small but positive.
